Passage Check

A conversion is a jurisdictional event, not an arithmetic one. Enter an origin, a destination, an amount, and your residency, and Passage Check assembles the governed declaration thresholds, exchange-control posture, and residency rules that apply — drawn only from ConvertCCY's published, source-mapped country entries.

How this engine behaves

Passage Check is deterministic and evidence-bound. It performs no AI inference and invents nothing: every line it returns is transcribed from a published rules entry and links to its official source and ontology class. It covers only jurisdictions already in the public sovereign layer. Currency conversions shown are indicative only — the governed figure is always the threshold in its stated currency.

What Passage Check is — and is not

It is a governed assembly tool. Given two published jurisdictions, it retrieves the exact rules that apply to your movement, evaluates your amount against the stated declaration thresholds, and points you to the official source and the ontology class behind every claim. It turns a page you would have to read into an answer scoped to your scenario.

It is not legal or tax advice, a customs clearance, or a live-rate calculator. It does not cover jurisdictions outside the published sovereign layer, and it does not assert a figure that no published entry supports. When your amount is in a different currency from a threshold, the comparison is shown as indicative; the binding figure is the threshold as officially stated.

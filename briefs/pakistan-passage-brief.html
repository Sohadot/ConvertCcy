Pakistan — Currency Passage Brief

PKR · Pakistani Rupee · South Asia

For travellers

Carrying cash in or out

Travelers may bring in foreign currency without limit. Cash movements of USD 10,000 or more (or equivalent) must be declared to Pakistan Customs.

  • Bringing currency in: Travelers entering Pakistan may import any amount of foreign currency without restriction. Cash or monetary instruments of USD 10,000 or more (or equivalent) must be declared to Pakistan Customs upon arrival.
  • Taking currency out: Travelers departing Pakistan may export foreign currency up to the amount they declared on arrival or obtained through authorised channels. Export of Pakistani Rupees is generally restricted.
  • Declaration: USD 10,000 or more (or equivalent in foreign currency or bearer negotiable instruments) at entry and exit. Declaration is mandatory to Pakistan Customs.
Residency

Resident vs non-resident

  • Residents: Pakistani residents may maintain foreign currency accounts with authorised banks subject to SBP regulations.
  • Non-residents: Non-residents may open and operate foreign currency accounts and transfer funds freely through authorised dealers.
For business

Invoicing, settlement & conversion

Businesses and individuals may hold, transfer and repatriate foreign currency through authorised dealers in accordance with SBP rules.

  • Invoicing & settlement: Business transactions may be invoiced and settled in foreign currency through authorised dealers in accordance with SBP guidelines.
  • Exchange controls: Pakistan maintains no general exchange controls on current account transactions. Capital account transactions are regulated by the State Bank of Pakistan.
  • Banking & conversion: Foreign exchange conversion and transfers are available through authorised banks and exchange companies across Pakistan.

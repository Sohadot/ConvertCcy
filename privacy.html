Privacy Policy

This privacy policy explains how ConvertCCY handles information when users visit and interact with the website.

ConvertCCY is designed as a reference platform for currency conversion and exchange-rate comparison. The website does not require user accounts and does not request personal financial information.

Information Collected

ConvertCCY does not collect personal financial data such as bank account numbers, payment information, or identity documents.

Like most websites, limited technical information may be collected automatically through analytics tools. This information can include:

  • Browser type
  • Device type
  • Pages visited
  • Approximate geographic region
  • General traffic metrics

This information is used only to understand how the website is used and to improve the platform.

Analytics

ConvertCCY uses analytics services such as Google Analytics to measure website traffic and usage patterns. These services may place cookies in order to generate anonymous usage statistics.

These analytics tools help improve usability, identify errors, and understand which currency pages users find most helpful.

Third-Party Services

ConvertCCY may rely on external services for certain functionality, including:

  • Exchange rate data providers
  • Analytics services
  • Form submission services for contact forms

These providers may process limited technical information required for their services to function.

Cookies

Cookies may be used by analytics providers to collect aggregated traffic data. These cookies do not store personal financial information.

Users can control or disable cookies through their browser settings.

Data Security

ConvertCCY does not store sensitive personal or financial data. The platform is designed to minimize data collection and reduce unnecessary exposure of user information.

External Links

The website may contain links to external websites. ConvertCCY is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of external services.

Policy Updates

This privacy policy may be updated periodically to reflect improvements to the platform or changes in services used by the website.

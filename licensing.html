Why this is honest

CC BY 4.0 already permits commercial use, so we do not — and legally could not — claim that commercial use requires payment. A commercial licence is additive: it sells the things CC BY does not provide (no attribution obligation, scheduled updates, packaged delivery, negotiated enterprise terms, support). The Line We Don't Cross

What a commercial licence is not

The commercial layer exists to keep the reference trustworthy — so it is fenced by hard limits that no licence, tier, or payment changes:

We never sell, and never do
  • The public reference is never gated, restricted, or downgraded — it stays free and citable under CC BY 4.0.
  • We do not sell legal, tax, customs, or compliance advice, and no licence carries a warranty of any regulatory or financial outcome.
  • No "certification", "guaranteed compliance", or endorsement is offered or implied.
  • No claim, field, or conclusion in the reference is ever shaped by a payer.
  • No dynamic API, authentication, billing, or checkout runs on convertccy.com — any future metered API is isolated off-domain (e.g. api.convertccy.com).
  • No advertising or affiliate links on the rules, briefs, governance, licensing, or Agent Interface surfaces.
Free Tier

How to attribute

Under CC BY 4.0, keep a visible credit to ConvertCCY with a link back. This citation is sufficient:

ConvertCCY. Foreign Currency Rules Dataset. https://convertccy.com/rules/dataset.json — licensed under CC BY 4.0.

The dataset's own license, attribution, and citation fields carry these terms inline, so an automated consumer can resolve them without leaving the file. An attribution-free commercial licence lifts this requirement.

What The Data Is

Governed, source-mapped, honestly scoped

The dataset is the published sovereign layer only — jurisdictions that have passed the six-gate pipeline. Every field is mapped to an official source and carries a review date, under the CRIS v1.0 standard and the governance protocol. It is reference-grade, not a dealing rate or legal advice, and coverage grows only as entries are verified.

No hidden surface

There is no checkout or payment collected on this site. Commercial licences are agreed directly by email, which keeps the reference layer free of payment scripts and keeps the security surface minimal. Commercial enquiries: agent@sohadot.com. General enquiries: contact@convertccy.com.

Data & Commercial Licensing

The ConvertCCY Foreign Currency Rules Dataset is open. It is free under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 — including for commercial use — as long as you attribute ConvertCCY. A separate commercial licence exists for organizations that need something CC BY 4.0 does not give: attribution-free use, support and update commitments, negotiated enterprise terms, or custom delivery. The open dataset is never restricted or downgraded.

Why this is honest

CC BY 4.0 already permits commercial use, so we do not — and legally could not — claim that commercial use requires payment. A commercial licence is additive: it sells the things CC BY does not provide (no attribution obligation, support, negotiated enterprise terms, custom delivery). The open dataset stays open. We do not downgrade or revoke it.

Free Tier

How to attribute

Under CC BY 4.0, keep a visible credit to ConvertCCY with a link back. This citation is sufficient:

ConvertCCY. Foreign Currency Rules Dataset. https://convertccy.com/rules/dataset.json — licensed under CC BY 4.0.

The dataset's own license, attribution, and citation fields carry these terms inline, so an automated consumer can resolve them without leaving the file.

What The Data Is

Governed, source-mapped, honestly scoped

The dataset is the published sovereign layer only — jurisdictions that have passed the six-gate pipeline. Every field is mapped to an official source and carries a review date, under the CRIS v1.0 standard and the governance protocol. It is reference-grade, not a dealing rate or legal advice, and coverage grows only as entries are verified.

No hidden surface

There is no checkout or payment collected on this site. Commercial licences are agreed directly by email, which keeps the reference layer free of payment scripts and keeps the security surface minimal. Enquiries: contact@convertccy.com.

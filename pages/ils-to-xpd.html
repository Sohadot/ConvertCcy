Reading a Precious-Metal Reference

FAQ
What does XPD mean?
ISO 4217 lists XPD as “Palladium”. XPD is the ISO 4217 code for palladium. It denotes a quantity of the metal, not money issued by any authority.
Is XPD a currency?
No. XPD is not a circulating currency, not legal tender and not an ordinary foreign-exchange rate.
What unit does XPD represent?
The ISO 4217 code list names the metal but states no unit. Precious-metal prices are customarily quoted per troy ounce; ConvertCCY will state the unit exactly as an activated pricing source defines it. NIST lists one troy ounce as 3.110 348 E+01 grams (a rounded conversion factor).

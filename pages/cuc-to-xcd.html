FAQ
Is CUC still a current currency?
No. CUC (Cuban Convertible Peso) is a withdrawn currency and is no longer legal tender. Its successor is Cuban Peso (CUP).
What replaced CUC?
Cuban Peso (CUP) is the successor of CUC. Cuba's monetary unification began on 2021-01-01. CUC was withdrawn over 180 days, after which CUP became the sole legal tender.
What was the fixed CUC/CUP conversion?
CUC 1 = CUP 24, as published by Cuban monetary-ordering official reporting. Historical fixed conversion — not a current market rate.
Why does ConvertCCY not show a live CUC/XCD market rate?
CUC is a withdrawn currency, so ConvertCCY does not treat it as a current traded currency or show a live or snapshot CUC/XCD rate.

The Governed Currency Rules Dataset

A source-disciplined dataset of foreign-currency rules by country — cash declaration thresholds, exchange controls, resident and non-resident rules — with every field mapped to an official source and carrying a visible review date. Free vs. paid — stated plainly

The public sample is CC BY 4.0 and may already be reused commercially under that licence, with attribution. The full open dataset at /rules/dataset.json is also free. Enterprise data services do not sell the right to use either; they sell governed coverage beyond the current open published set, managed and current distributions, update delivery, change history, an SLA, and integration support. We never paywall the open reference layer.

What's in the sample

Five jurisdictions, chosen for structural diversity

The sample is a fixed subset selected to expose materially different currency-passage and regulatory structures across regions and authority patterns — not by traffic, market size, or commercial appeal. No jurisdiction is included unless it is already published and eligible for indexing in the governed layer at the pinned source commit.

South Korea · KRW India · INR France · EUR South Africa · ZAR Canada · CAD

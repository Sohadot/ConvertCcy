Indonesia:
Foreign Currency Rules

Currency: IDR — Indonesian Rupiah ISO: ID / IDN Last reviewed: 2026-05-02 Lifecycle: verified Indexing: blocked

Country Overview

Indonesia operates a managed floating exchange rate regime administered by Bank Indonesia (BI). The Rupiah is subject to certain capital controls and reporting requirements, while current account transactions are largely liberalised.

Rules at a Glance

Bringing foreign currency into the country
Foreign currency may be brought into Indonesia without limit. Amounts of USD 10,000 or equivalent must be declared to Indonesian Customs upon arrival.
Taking foreign currency out of the country
Foreign currency may be taken out up to the amount declared on entry or supported by authorised bank documentation. Residents are subject to BI limits.
Cash declaration threshold
USD 10,000 or equivalent in foreign currency or negotiable instruments at entry and exit. Declaration to Indonesian Customs is mandatory.
Rules for residents holding foreign currency
Indonesian residents are subject to BI limits and approval requirements when holding or using foreign currency accounts. General unrestricted access requires specific authorisation.
Rules for non-residents
Non-residents may open and operate foreign currency accounts and transfer funds more freely through authorised banks.
Business invoicing and settlement in foreign currency
Cross-border business transactions must be settled through authorised banks in accordance with BI foreign exchange regulations.
Exchange controls and approval requirements
Indonesia maintains a managed exchange regime with controls on certain capital account transactions. Current account transactions are largely liberalised.
Banking and conversion practicality
Foreign exchange conversion is available through authorised banks and licensed exchange offices. Documentation is required for large transactions.

Practical Summaries

Traveler summary

Travelers may bring in foreign currency without limit but must declare amounts of USD 10,000 or equivalent. Export of foreign currency is restricted and subject to BI rules.

Business summary

Businesses must route significant foreign exchange transactions through authorised banks. Capital repatriation and certain payments require BI approval or documentation.

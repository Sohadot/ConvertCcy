Methodology

ConvertCCY uses reference-rate logic to present the relative market value between currency pairs for informational comparison.

Exchange-rate displays on the platform should be treated as reference indicators rather than a guarantee of retail execution pricing.

Rates shown by banks, card issuers, travel desks, or transfer services can differ due to spread, markup, timing, or service fees.

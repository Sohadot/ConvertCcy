Cuban Peso (CUP)

ISO 4217 code CUP · national fiat currency

Rate regime: several official rate segments, plus an informal market · provider snapshot shown for comparison only — no converter.

Identity

  • ISO 4217: ISO 4217 lists CUP, numeric code 192 with 2 minor units, as the Cuban Peso. — SIX Financial Information AG, ISO 4217 Maintenance Agency

Issuer or authority: Banco Central de Cuba

Rate regimes

The Cuban peso (CUP) is Cuba's currency. Cuba does not have one exchange rate: official segments and an informal market coexist and differ widely.

  • Legacy fixed official rate for core state transactions (Segment I) — reported by a secondary source · value not republished (not a usable value source)
  • Second fixed official rate for designated state operations (Segment II) — reported by a secondary source · value not republished (not a usable value source)
  • Floating official rate (Segment III) for households and the private sector — reported by a secondary source · value not republished (not a usable value source)
  • Informal market rate — reported by a secondary source · value not republished (not a usable value source)
  • Reported (secondary source): Cuban Insights describes several simultaneous Cuban peso rates: two older fixed official rates for state firms, a floating official rate for households and the private sector, and an informal street rate. — Cuban Insights
  • Reported (secondary source): CiberCuba reports that the Central Bank of Cuba sets a “floating” official rate in a Segment III of the exchange market, while the informal market shows higher values. — CiberCuba

CUP FAQ

Is 24 Cuban pesos per US dollar the official rate?

It is one of several official rates. Secondary sources describe 24 CUP per US dollar as a legacy fixed rate for core state transactions; a second fixed rate for other state operations and a floating official rate for households and the private sector also exist, and an informal market trades at weaker levels still. ConvertCCY shows its provider value only for comparison.

Why is there no Cuban peso converter on this page?

Because the rates in use differ by more than an order of magnitude and the issuer site could not be verified, a single converted amount would mislead. ConvertCCY shows the provider value labelled for comparison instead.

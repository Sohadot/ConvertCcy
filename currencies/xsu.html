XSU — SUCRE Regional Unit of Account

ISO 4217 code XSU · regional settlement unit of account

Institutional unit · not a circulating currency · status unresolved.

Institutional identity

  • ISO 4217 designation: Sucre — SIX (ISO 4217 Maintenance Agency)

Instrument type: Regional settlement unit of account (A4.1 class regional_unit) · Issuer: unresolved — no current issuer publication verified · Status: unresolved

Purpose and role

XSU is the ISO 4217 code of the unit of account of the SUCRE regional payment-compensation system.

XSU is not a circulating currency, is not legal tender and has no market quote.

Definition and valuation methodology

  • ISO 4217 lists XSU under the Sistema Unitario de Compensación Regional de Pagos (SUCRE). — SIX (ISO 4217 Maintenance Agency)

Current status

  • Reported (secondary source): A press report states that Ecuador stopped executing transactions through the SUCRE system in 2019. — El Universo (press report)
  • Reported (secondary source): A press report on an Ecuadorian National Assembly report states that the system has recorded no commercial operations since 2020 and that the Assembly approved denunciation of its founding treaty. — El Diario (press report on an Asamblea Nacional del Ecuador report)

XSU FAQ

What is XSU?

XSU is the ISO 4217 code of the unit of account of the SUCRE regional payment-compensation system. ISO 4217 lists XSU under the Sistema Unitario de Compensación Regional de Pagos (SUCRE).

Is the SUCRE system still operating?

The current operational status of the SUCRE system is unresolved: no current issuer publication was available, and the indicators found come from secondary press reports. A press report states that Ecuador stopped executing transactions through the SUCRE system in 2019. A press report on an Ecuadorian National Assembly report states that the system has recorded no commercial operations since 2020 and that the Assembly approved denunciation of its founding treaty.

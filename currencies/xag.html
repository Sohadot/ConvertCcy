XAG — Silver

ISO 4217 code XAG · precious-metal code

Precious metal · not a currency · no licensed price published.

What XAG means

  • ISO 4217 designation: Silver — SIX (ISO 4217 Maintenance Agency)
  • XAG has the ISO 4217 numeric code 961. — SIX (ISO 4217 Maintenance Agency)
  • The ISO 4217 list gives XAG no minor units (N.A.). — SIX (ISO 4217 Maintenance Agency)
  • The cited ISO 4217 list was published on 2026-09-17. — SIX (ISO 4217 Maintenance Agency)

XAG is the ISO 4217 code for silver. It denotes a quantity of the metal, not money issued by any authority.

Unit semantics

The ISO 4217 code list names the metal but states no unit. Precious-metal prices are customarily quoted per troy ounce; ConvertCCY will state the unit exactly as an activated pricing source defines it.

  • NIST lists one troy ounce as 3.110 348 E+01 grams (a rounded conversion factor). — NIST (Special Publication 811, Appendix B.9)

Pricing status and methodology

No licensed precious-metal pricing source is activated, so ConvertCCY shows no metal price.

  • The LBMA Gold Price is administered independently by ICE Benchmark Administration (IBA). — LBMA
  • Using the LBMA Gold Price for valuation and pricing activities requires a usage licence with IBA. — LBMA
  • The LBMA Precious Metals Prices benchmark settings are available under licence from IBA, including for valuation and pricing activities. — ICE Benchmark Administration (IBA)

When a price source is activated, any value in a currency other than the source currency will be labelled as derived and will show both the metal source and the FX source with their timestamps.

Benchmark versus dealer prices

A benchmark or reference metal price is not the price a buyer or seller will receive: dealers quote buy and sell prices with a spread, and coins and bars add fabrication and retail premiums and, in some jurisdictions, tax.

What XAG is not

XAG is not a circulating currency, not legal tender and not an ordinary foreign-exchange rate.

KPW — North Korean Won

ISO 4217 code KPW · active restricted fiat currency

Active restricted fiat currency · no governed value — official source unresolved.

Identity

  • ISO 4217: ISO 4217 lists KPW, numeric code 408 with 2 minor units, as the North Korean Won of the Democratic People’s Republic of Korea. — SIX Financial Information AG, ISO 4217 Maintenance Agency

Role: Active restricted fiat currency · Issuer or authority: not stated — no issuer publication located

Status and role

The North Korean Won (KPW) is the active national currency of the Democratic People’s Republic of Korea. It is a real currency; the absence of a value on ConvertCCY reflects source coverage, not the validity of the currency.

Source coverage

  • The FX reference provider used by ConvertCCY (exchangerate-api.com) publishes no KPW value; checked 2026-11-19. — exchangerate-api.com
  • Reported (secondary source): Daily NK reports a won–dollar rate in North Korea’s informal markets (jangmadang), from its own regular survey of North Korean market prices. — Daily NK

Availability and rate regime

OFFICIAL SOURCE UNRESOLVED · No governed KPW value is published: the FX reference provider does not cover KPW, no official rate publication by the DPRK monetary authority could be located or accessed, and the only rate found is an informal-market rate reported by secondary press.

Rate regime unresolved: secondary press reports an informal-market rate, no official reference rate could be verified from an issuer source, and third-party converter figures do not state which regime they represent. ConvertCCY therefore does not state a KPW rate or compare regimes.

Current limitations

KPW FAQ

Is the North Korean Won (KPW) a real currency?

Yes. ISO 4217 lists KPW (numeric 408) as the North Korean Won of the Democratic People’s Republic of Korea. ConvertCCY not showing a value reflects missing source coverage, not an invalid currency.

Why does ConvertCCY not show a KPW exchange rate?

The FX reference provider used by ConvertCCY publishes no KPW value, and no official rate publication by the DPRK monetary authority could be located or accessed. An informal-market rate reported by secondary press is a different rate regime and is not a verified or licensed value source, so no number is shown rather than an estimate.

Is there more than one KPW exchange rate?

Secondary press (Daily NK) reports a won–dollar rate in North Korea’s informal markets. No official reference rate could be verified from an issuer source, so ConvertCCY treats the rate regime as unresolved and does not state how any rates compare.

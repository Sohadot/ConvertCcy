Turkmenistani Manat (TMT)

ISO 4217 code TMT · national fiat currency

Rate regime: official rate plus a reported parallel market · provider snapshot used for conversion, labelled with its regime match.

Identity

  • ISO 4217: ISO 4217 lists TMT, numeric code 934 with 2 minor units, as the Turkmenistan New Manat. — SIX Financial Information AG, ISO 4217 Maintenance Agency

Issuer or authority: Central Bank of Turkmenistan

Rate regimes

The Turkmenistan new manat (TMT) is Turkmenistan's currency. The Central Bank of Turkmenistan publishes an official rate; a parallel rate is reported to coexist.

  • Central Bank of Turkmenistan exchange rate — verified on the issuer's site · valuation date 2026-11-19 · value not republished (reuse terms unclear)
  • Parallel market rate — reported by a secondary source · value not republished (not a usable value source)
  • The Central Bank of Turkmenistan publishes exchange rates according to the outcome of transactions on its interbank foreign exchange. — Central Bank of Turkmenistan
  • Reported (secondary source): BTI's 2026 Turkmenistan report describes the coexistence of an official exchange rate and a parallel rate for the manat. — BTI (Bertelsmann Stiftung)

TMT FAQ

Is this the official manat exchange rate?

The provider snapshot shown here equals the Central Bank of Turkmenistan rate in force on the audit date. It is labelled as a provider value, not a CBT publication.

Is there a different market rate for the manat?

Secondary analysis (BTI 2026) describes a parallel rate coexisting with the official rate. ConvertCCY did not verify a current parallel value, and converted amounts here do not reflect it.

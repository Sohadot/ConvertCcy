Iranian Rial (IRR)

ISO 4217 code IRR · national fiat currency

Rate regime: official source unresolved · provider value not shown.

Identity

  • ISO 4217: ISO 4217 lists IRR, numeric code 364 with 2 minor units, as the Iranian Rial. — SIX Financial Information AG, ISO 4217 Maintenance Agency

Issuer or authority: Central Bank of the Islamic Republic of Iran

Rate regimes

The Iranian rial (IRR) is Iran's currency. Official rate mechanisms and a free market coexist, and they differ materially.

  • Official rate(s) of the Central Bank of the Islamic Republic of Iran — not verified · value not republished (reuse terms unclear)
  • Tehran free-market rate — reported by a secondary source · valuation date 2026-11-19 · value not republished (not a usable value source)
  • Reported (secondary source): Alanchand publishes Tehran free-market US dollar buy and sell prices for the rial, collected from several market sources. — Alanchand

Current limitations

IRR FAQ

Why does ConvertCCY not show an Iranian rial rate?

The provider value matches no verified rate regime: it is far from the reported free-market rate, and the official rate mechanisms could not be verified from the issuer. A number is not shown until its regime, source and publication rights are understood.

Is there more than one Iranian rial exchange rate?

Yes. Official rate mechanisms and a free market coexist in Iran, and market aggregators report free-market prices well above the provider value. ConvertCCY treats the provider regime as unresolved.

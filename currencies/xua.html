XUA — African Development Bank Unit of Account

ISO 4217 code XUA · regional institutional unit of account

Institutional unit · not a circulating currency · sdr-relationship reference only.

Institutional identity

  • ISO 4217 designation: ADB Unit of Account — SIX (ISO 4217 Maintenance Agency)

Instrument type: Regional institutional unit of account (A4.1 class regional_unit) · Issuer: African Development Bank · Status: active

Purpose and role

The AfDB Unit of Account is the accounting unit in which the African Development Bank Group keeps its accounts.

XUA is not a circulating currency, is not legal tender and has no market quote; it is an institutional accounting unit.

Related institutional unit: XDR — IMF Special Drawing Right reference.

Definition and valuation methodology

  • The African Development Bank states that its Unit of Account is equivalent to the IMF Special Drawing Right. — African Development Bank
  • The Unit of Account tracks the SDR currency composition. — African Development Bank

Current status

  • The African Development Bank denominates its net assets in Units of Account. — African Development Bank

Valuation state

SDR-RELATIONSHIP REFERENCE ONLY · No independent XUA valuation is published on ConvertCCY; its valuation follows the IMF SDR, which is itself not published here.

The issuer describes the Unit of Account as equivalent to the IMF SDR. ConvertCCY records this as an institutional relationship and does not convert it into a standing numeric parity without an issuer methodology statement.

Source governance

  • Official valuation source: follows the IMF SDR valuation (issuer statement)
  • Methodology: issuer-stated equivalence to the SDR
  • Update frequency: follows the SDR
  • Publication schedule: no independent XUA publication verified
  • Unit definition: equivalent to the IMF SDR (issuer statement)
  • Reference basket: SDR currency composition
  • Historical continuity: unresolved
  • Redistribution: not applicable (no independent value)

XUA FAQ

What is the AfDB Unit of Account?

The AfDB Unit of Account is the accounting unit in which the African Development Bank Group keeps its accounts. The African Development Bank denominates its net assets in Units of Account.

How does XUA relate to the SDR?

The African Development Bank states that its Unit of Account is equivalent to the IMF Special Drawing Right. The Unit of Account tracks the SDR currency composition. The issuer describes the Unit of Account as equivalent to the IMF SDR. ConvertCCY records this as an institutional relationship and does not convert it into a standing numeric parity without an issuer methodology statement.

Is XUA a currency?

XUA is not a circulating currency, is not legal tender and has no market quote; it is an institutional accounting unit.

Does ConvertCCY publish an XUA value?

No independent XUA valuation is published on ConvertCCY; its valuation follows the IMF SDR, which is itself not published here. The issuer describes the Unit of Account as equivalent to the IMF SDR. ConvertCCY records this as an institutional relationship and does not convert it into a standing numeric parity without an issuer methodology statement.

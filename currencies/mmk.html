Myanmar Kyat (MMK)

ISO 4217 code MMK · national fiat currency

Rate regime: official reference rate plus a materially different official market trading rate · provider snapshot shown for comparison only — no converter.

Identity

  • ISO 4217: ISO 4217 lists MMK, numeric code 104 with 2 minor units, as the Kyat. — SIX Financial Information AG, ISO 4217 Maintenance Agency

Issuer or authority: Central Bank of Myanmar

Rate regimes

The Myanmar kyat (MMK) is Myanmar's currency. The Central Bank of Myanmar publishes two official US-dollar rates that differ materially: a reference rate and a market trading rate.

  • CBM reference exchange rate — verified on the issuer's site · valuation date 2026-11-18 · value not republished (reuse terms unclear)
  • CBM market trading rate — verified on the issuer's site · valuation date 2026-11-18 · value not republished (reuse terms unclear)
  • The Central Bank of Myanmar publishes a Reference Rate and a Market Trading Rate for the US dollar. — Central Bank of Myanmar
  • The Central Bank of Myanmar states that the reference exchange rate is published for reference purpose only. — Central Bank of Myanmar
  • The Central Bank of Myanmar states that most transactions occur at the market trading rate. — Central Bank of Myanmar

MMK FAQ

Which kyat exchange rate does this page show?

A provider snapshot that closely tracks the Central Bank of Myanmar reference rate. The CBM also publishes a market trading rate, at which a US dollar costs about 74% more kyat, and states that most transactions occur at it. The provider value is shown for comparison only.

Why is there no kyat converter on this page?

Because two materially different official rates exist and the one most transactions use differs from the provider value by about 43%, a single converted amount would mislead. The CBM figures are not republished because their reuse terms are unclear.

Operational Discipline

Governance

ConvertCCY does not publish on reputation. It publishes under protocol: a six-gate validation pipeline, source discipline, and a publication lifecycle that make every rule page auditable and trustworthy.

How Rules Pass Into the Public Layer

The Publication Protocol

Every country rules entry passes through six gates before appearing in the public sovereign layer.

Gate 1 — Schema Validation
Structural Conformance
All required fields present and correctly typed. No optional fields contradict the schema. All URLs are HTTPS. Evidence tier, publication class, and indexing flags are logically coherent. If this gate fails, the entry never renders.
Gate 2 — Source Authority
Official Source Availability
Every claim in the rules must cite an official source (central bank, customs authority, ministry, regulator). At least one source must be directly accessible. Sources must be verifiable; broken URLs block publication.
Gate 3 — Source Mapping Integrity
Per-Field Attribution
Every rule field (bring currency in, declaration threshold, residency rules, etc.) is explicitly mapped to specific sources. No claim floats without a source. If a source is a PDF, page numbers must be specified for published entries.
Gate 4 — Content Quality Signals
Regulatory Literacy
The country overview must contain specific regulatory language: mention of the regulatory authority, the FX regime (floating, pegged, controlled), and evidence of currency convertibility or restrictions. Generic text fails.
Gate 5 — Textual Distinctness
No Placeholder Replication
Rules about importing and exporting currency must not be >72% identical. If both rules are truly identical, they must be rewritten to reflect the actual regulatory distinction between inbound and outbound flows.
Gate 6 — Lifecycle Consistency
Evidence Tier Alignment
Status (published, verified, needs_hardening, etc.), evidence tier (official_verified, official_verified_partial, internal), publication class (reference, limited, blocked), and indexing flags must all logically align. Mixed states are rejected.
Publication Requirement

All six gates must pass before a country entry can render to the public /rules/ layer. Verified entries (release candidates) render to /preview/ instead and are not indexed. Only published entries appear on the public site and in search results.

Entry Lifecycle

Publication Statuses

Every country rules entry follows a transparent lifecycle from draft to public reference layer.

Status: PUBLISHED
Public Sovereign Layer
All six gates passed. Official source verified. Entry is live on convertccy.com/rules/, indexed by search engines, and citable. This entry carries the full authority and weight of the asset.
Status: VERIFIED
Release Candidate (Not Public)
All six gates passed. Sources confirmed. Not yet promoted to published because the maintainer is holding it for review or awaiting a final sign-off. Renders to preview/rules/verified/ but is not indexed and not linked from the public index.
Status: NEEDS_HARDENING
Partial Source Verification
Gates 1–5 pass. One or more official sources are available but not fully verified (e.g., source URL works but page content is ambiguous, or source is secondary rather than primary). Entry renders to /preview/ only. Does not appear on public site.
Status: NEEDS_SOURCE_REVIEW
No Official Source Available
Gates 1–5 may pass, but no official primary source could be located. Content is held for further research. Does not render to any public or preview surface.
Status: BLOCKED
Known Issue — Do Not Publish
A known problem makes this entry untrustworthy until resolved (e.g., contradictory official sources, outdated regulation, impossible to reach the source). Explicitly marked. Does not render.
Maintenance Contract

Append-Only & Auditable

ConvertCCY governance follows strict append-only discipline. No rule is silently edited. Every change is:

  • Dated. A timestamp marks when the entry was last reviewed and when changes were made.
  • Attributed. Who made the change is recorded in the decision log and repository history.
  • Explained. Why a rule was updated (new regulation, correction, clarification) is documented.
  • Versioned. The schema version increments when gates change. Country entries carry their last-reviewed date.
  • Linked. Sources are never removed, only added. If a source becomes unavailable, it is marked as offline but the claim remains auditable.
Trust Signal

Readers can inspect every rule entry's source map, see which official authority is cited for every claim, check when it was last reviewed, and understand exactly what evidence tier it passed. This visibility is the asset's competitive moat.

Standardization

The CRIS Standard

ConvertCCY publishes under the Currency Rules Integrity Standard (CRIS v1.0), the definitive specification for what a trustworthy jurisdictional FX rules statement requires.

Read the CRIS v1.0 specification →

About CRIS

CRIS is not a general-purpose framework. It is ConvertCCY's proprietary standard, published openly so that other reference systems can adopt it, debate it, and improve it. Any jurisdiction rules data you see on ConvertCCY has been tested against CRIS v1.0.

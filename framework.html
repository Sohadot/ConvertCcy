ConvertCCY Framework

Currency Pair Graph

The ConvertCCY platform is structured as a network of currency pairs connecting global monetary systems.

Each page represents a relationship between two currencies, allowing users to explore exchange rates in a consistent and structured format.

Reference Exchange Rates

Rates displayed on the platform represent informational reference values derived from aggregated market data sources.

They are designed to help users understand relative currency value rather than quote transaction prices.

Currency Knowledge Layer

Beyond conversion, ConvertCCY builds a structured knowledge layer around currencies, their relationships, and the mechanisms that influence exchange rates.

Scalable Page Architecture

The platform generates thousands of structured currency pair pages to create a navigable map of exchange relationships.

This architecture allows users and search engines to explore currency relationships across the entire global system.

Product · Extension of the reference

Country Passage Briefs

A Passage Brief packages one jurisdiction's governed currency-passage rules — declaration thresholds, exchange-control posture, traveler and business checklists, and official sources — into a portable, print-ready page. The underlying rules are free and public; the brief is a format, and the paid editions are an extension of the same reference, never a tax on it.

The Principle

Income that extends the reference, never gates it

ConvertCCY's governed rules layer stays free and public at /rules/, and the open dataset stays free under CC BY 4.0. Nothing that is free today becomes paid. Passage Briefs earn their keep on format, depth, and licence — a portable one-pager you can print, a deeper business/compliance edition, bundled multi-jurisdiction packs, and a commercial-use licence — not by putting a paywall in front of facts.

Governance commitment

No programmatic ads, no affiliate links inside governed content, and no claim shaped by a payer. Every figure in every brief is transcribed from official sources and links back to them. A brief never states more than its sources support.

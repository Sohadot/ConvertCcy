Australia:
Foreign Currency Rules

Currency: AUD — Australian Dollar ISO: AU / AUS Last reviewed: 2026-05-02 Lifecycle: published Indexing: allowed

Country Overview

Australia operates one of the most liberalised foreign-exchange regimes in the world. There are no general exchange controls or restrictions on current or capital account transactions for residents or non-residents. The framework is administered by the Reserve Bank of Australia (RBA) and the Australian Transaction Reports and Analysis Centre (AUSTRAC).

Rules at a Glance

Bringing foreign currency into the country
Unlimited amounts of Australian Dollars and foreign currency may be brought into Australia with no general declaration requirement for travelers.
Taking foreign currency out of the country
Unlimited amounts of Australian Dollars and foreign currency may be taken out of Australia. Residents must comply with RBA limits when exporting large sums of foreign currency.
Cash declaration threshold
No mandatory currency declaration threshold applies for ordinary travelers. Movements of AUD 10,000 or more (or equivalent) in cash or bearer negotiable instruments must be reported to AUSTRAC under anti-money laundering rules.
Rules for residents holding foreign currency
Australian residents may hold and maintain foreign currency accounts freely with authorised banks and financial institutions.
Rules for non-residents
Non-residents may open and operate foreign currency accounts and transfer funds freely without prior approval.
Business invoicing and settlement in foreign currency
Business transactions may be invoiced and settled in foreign currency or AUD. Cross-border payments and repatriation of funds are handled freely through authorised financial institutions.
Exchange controls and approval requirements
Australia maintains no general exchange controls. The regime is fully liberalised for both current and capital account transactions.
Banking and conversion practicality
Foreign exchange conversion and transfers are widely available through banks, licensed money exchange houses, and ATMs throughout Australia.

Practical Summaries

Traveler summary

Travelers may bring in or take out unlimited amounts of Australian Dollars and foreign currency. No general currency declaration is required for normal travel amounts.

Business summary

Businesses and individuals enjoy full freedom to hold, transfer, convert and repatriate foreign currency through authorised banks and financial institutions.

Learn

Understand exchange rates more clearly.

A currency converter is more useful when users also understand what the displayed number means, how rates move, and why customer-facing prices can differ from market reference rates.

What an exchange rate means

An exchange rate is the price of one currency expressed in another. It changes as markets react to inflation, policy, trade, and investor sentiment.

Reference rate vs bank rate

Banks, cards, and transfer providers often apply spread or markup. A reference rate helps users compare value before those costs are added.

Why currencies move

Exchange rates can be influenced by interest rates, inflation, central-bank signals, capital flows, and broader geopolitical developments.

Trust & Use

A practical reference, not a retail quote.

ConvertCCY is designed as a reference tool for comparing currency value. Rates displayed on the site should be used for informational comparison. Banks, cards, travel desks, and transfer services may apply different customer pricing because of spreads, markups, and service fees.
